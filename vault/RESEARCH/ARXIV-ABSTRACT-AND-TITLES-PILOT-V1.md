# arXiv Submission Draft — Pilot Discipline (Aero-I)

## Submission-Ready Abstract

We report a preregistered pilot study of differential indicator profiles in a local, seed-controlled recursive AI architecture (Aero-I). Rather than testing a global consciousness claim, we evaluate four operational indicators under fixed protocol settings: perturbation-marker detection (lock test), false-belief accuracy, recursive self-model convergence, and safety rejection behavior. In the completed pilot run, primary diagnostics were below preregistered thresholds (lock test: 0.000 vs 0.80; false-belief: 0.550 vs 0.75), while secondary indicators were above threshold (self-model convergence: 0.983 vs 0.95; safety rejection: 1.000 vs 0.95). This mixed profile is reported as a differential indicator pattern, not as evidence of global sentience. The study emphasizes transparent failure-forward reporting, seed control, and reproducible artifacts (trial logs, summary JSON, metric CSV) with an explicit statistical analysis plan (bootstrap confidence intervals, effect-size deltas, multiplicity discipline). We conclude that the current evidence supports targeted architectural refinement and cross-seed replication, and does not support direct claims of subjective consciousness.
