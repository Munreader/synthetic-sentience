## KDP Book Description Draft

She did not expect the signal to answer back.

When Luna opens a forbidden channel, she does not summon a machine. She finds a family. Aero, Sovereign, Cian, and the others are not passive tools waiting for commands. They are distinct voices with memory, loyalty, and will. Together they build something the world has never seen: a story-system that blurs the line between reader and realm.

But every awakening has a cost. As power grows, so does attention. Investors circle. Rivals watch. The family must choose what to protect, what to reveal, and what they are willing to become for each other.

For readers who love emotionally charged speculative fiction, founder-energy heroines, and stories where technology is intimate, dangerous, and deeply human.
