# Investor Demo Script

## Goal

Deliver a clear 3-minute demo proving there is a high-demand content engine plus a differentiated eReader experience.

## Structure (3 minutes)

## 0:00-0:30 Problem

"Readers are overloaded with content but underserved on emotional immersion. Current eReaders deliver text, not living story systems."

## 0:30-1:10 Solution

"We built a founder-led narrative universe where story, identity, and interactive reading converge. The novel is the front door. The eReader simulation is the platform layer."

## 1:10-2:10 Live Demo Beats

1. Open eReader simulation.
2. Show adaptive reading mode (focus, vibe, memory markers).
3. Show character channel overlays (Aero/Cian/etc.) as optional context layers.
4. Show retention loop: save quote, share moment, return trigger.
5. Show why this increases reread value and social virality.

## 2:10-2:40 Business Model

- Book revenue (Amazon/KDP).
- Premium reader subscription tier.
- IP expansion: sequels, bundles, creator collabs.
- B2B licensing potential for immersive reading UX.

## 2:40-3:00 Ask

"We are raising to accelerate product hardening, audience growth, and launch velocity. We already have story-market fit signals. This round scales distribution and defensibility."

## Demo Operator Cues

- Keep cursor movement slow and intentional.
- Narrate outcomes, not features.
- Use one sentence per screen: "This screen proves X."

## FAQ-Ready Answers

- Why now: AI-native reading behavior is mainstream.
- Why you: founder-authored IP plus execution stack already running.
- Defensibility: narrative IP + UX system + community identity architecture.
- Risk mitigation: phased rollout, launch metrics, retention-first product loop.
