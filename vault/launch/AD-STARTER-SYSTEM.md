# Ad Starter System

## Objective

Acquire first 1,000 high-intent readers while validating best-converting message angles.

## Core Audience Segments

1. Women who read sci-fi romance and fantasy romance.
2. Founder and creator women interested in identity and power narratives.
3. AI-curious readers who enjoy near-future emotional fiction.
4. BookTok and Instagram Reels fiction discoverers.
5. Readers of cinematic, character-driven speculative stories.

## 14-Day Test Plan

- Days 1-3: test 5 hooks x 2 creatives each.
- Days 4-7: cut bottom 50 percent by CTR and hold-rate.
- Days 8-10: scale top 3 ad sets by 20-30 percent daily.
- Days 11-14: optimize landing page and run retargeting.

## KPIs

- CTR target: >= 1.5 percent
- CPC target: <= $1.20
- Landing conversion target: >= 8 percent
- Email capture target: >= 20 percent of visitors
